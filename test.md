# AthenaSummary Brush-up – Quick Reference

A one-page summary of the brush-up rules in [`README.md`](README.md). If this
page and the README disagree, the README wins.

## Goal

Turn a writer's draft into a finished, sellable AthenaSummary: student-focused,
**no signs of AI**, and consistent in writing, visuals and formatting.

## Rule priority

1. `README.md` / `03-DECISIONS.md`
2. Handbook (`00`, `01`, `02`)
3. Gold example summaries
4. Writer Manual (August 2026)
5. Master template

## Delivery

- Deliver **only the finished .docx**: no tracked changes, comments, findings
  table or grade.
- File name: `<UNI>-<PROG>-<SKU>-2627-<week number>.docx`
  (e.g. `RU-PSY-1033-2627-4.docx`).
- Ask for the syllabus or old exams only when really needed.

## Week layout

| Order | Part | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Week title (Heading 1) | Short and general |
| 2 | White week box | Intro sentence, "I know" checklist, pie chart |
| 3 | Grey Hook Question box | `Hook Question` in bold, options not answered |
| 4 | Content | Lectures, chapters, boxes |
| 5 | Grey answer box | Same question and option order, correct answer and why |

- Week 1 has a title page; no table of contents or Exam Analysis page.
- Page numbers start at 1 in every file.
- No checklist or pie chart at the end of a week; no "N" or "X" placeholders.

## Headings

| Draft has | Lecture | Chapter | Section | Sub-section |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lecture + chapter | H2 | H3 | H4 | Bold text |
| Chapter only | – | H2 | H3 | H4 |

Format: `Week 4 – Title`, `Lecture 7 – Title`, `Chapter 9 – Title`, `9.1 Title`.
Fonts: H1 Calibri 16 (new page), H2 14, H3 12, H4 11, all bold.

## Layout

- A4, 2.5 cm margins, Calibri 11, justified, single spacing.
- Logo: odd pages right, even pages left. Footer: line with centred page number.
- No empty lines or manual page breaks; nothing splits across pages.
- Real Word lists only. **Bold** key terms, *italics* for stress, no underlining.

## Writing

- UK spelling. Fix typos directly.
- Body text is neutral: no "you" or "we", no sentences starting with
  "There", "It" or "Therefore". Boxes may use "you".
- No long dashes (—), linking phrases, source references or over-explaining.
- Paragraphs at least 4 lines. Cut repetition, never a unique topic.

## Boxes

One-cell table, full width, thin black border, bold label, black icon top right.

| Box | Label | Fill |
|---|---|---|
| Example | `Example:` | Grey D0CECE |
| Tip | `Tip!` | White |
| Please note | `Please note!` | White |
| Stop and think | `Stop and think!` | White |
| Mnemonic | `Mnemonic:` | White |
| Hook | `Hook Question` | Grey |

- Aim for 2+ boxes per page, using all 5 types.
- Names: Adrian, Xavier, Matko, Thisaru, Amber, Elisa (no pronouns).
- Mnemonic first letters purple and bold (`7A4E8C`).

## Visuals and colours

- Remake every diagram in the purple palette; images centred, max 16 × 12 cm.
- Tables: header `AA7FB8` bold, banding `DDBDFD`, first column `7A4E8C` bold.

## Dutch summaries

Make every change in Dutch. Labels: Voorbeeld:, Tip!, Let op!, Denk na!,
Ezelsbruggetje:, Hoofdstuk, Hoorcollege.

## Final check

Before sending, scroll through every page and run the checklist in the
README's [Final check](README.md#final-check) section.
